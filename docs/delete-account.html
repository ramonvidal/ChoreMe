In the app

  1. Open Nos Corre! and go to the Family tab.
  2. Scroll to Your data.
  3. Tap Leave family to remove this phone from the family (your location, notification address and arrival history are deleted; chores and groceries you added stay for the family), or Delete my data to also delete your account. If you are the last member, the whole family's data is deleted too.

What is deleted

Your account, your member profile, your location, your notification address and your arrival history. Messages you sent can be deleted by you or by a parent. Chores and groceries you added remain for the family unless the family is deleted.

Without the app

If you can no longer open the app, open an issue on the project page asking for deletion, with the name you used in your family and the family's name. Please do not post anything private there. We will answer with how to confirm that you own the account.

No app (Português)

No app: aba Família → Seus dados → Sair da família ou Apagar meus dados (apaga também sua conta; se você for o último membro, a família inteira é apagada). Sem acesso ao app, abra um chamado pedindo a exclusão, com o nome que você usava e o nome da família, sem colocar nada privado.
